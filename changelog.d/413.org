* Added
- #413 =org-canvas-validate= has a third level beside errors and
  warnings: a /note/, for a course that works as written but may not be
  laid out as a student needs.  Notes are listed after the rest, counted
  apart (=1 error(s), 3 warning(s), 9 note(s)=), and never fail
  =org-canvas-validate-batch=.
- #413 =org-canvas-validate= notes every published heading in
  =assignments.org=, =pages.org=, =quizzes.org= and =discussions.org=
  that no =modules.org= item links to.  Unpublished headings, the front
  page, deleted headings and headings marked =NO_MODULE: t= are left
  out.  =org-canvas-validate-module-coverage= names the files checked
  (add =org-canvas-files-file= for files, or nil to turn it off).
