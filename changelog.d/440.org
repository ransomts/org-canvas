* Added
- #440 Two commands resolve a grading file's =CONFLICT= on the heading
  at point, after reading the student's submission: =t=
  (=org-canvas-submissions-take-canvas=) makes =SCORE=, =LATE_STATUS=
  and the =Rubric= rows Canvas's and keeps what was typed as one line
  under =Notes=; =k= (=org-canvas-submissions-keep-mine=) keeps the
  typed grade and makes Canvas's its baseline, so the next push sends
  it.  A refresh's closing line names every student it left marked
  =CONFLICT=, with the reason.

* Fixed
- #440 A heading marked =CONFLICT= is no longer pushed.  After a
  refresh re-baselined a row to a newer SpeedGrader grade, the next
  push sent the file's older typed score (or Rubric rows) over it.
  The push now holds the whole heading (score, rows, late status,
  drafted comment) until =t= or =k= resolves it, names it in its
  closing message, and returns =:conflict-names= beside =:conflicts=,
  which counts every heading held back.
