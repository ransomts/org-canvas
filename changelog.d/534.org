* Added
- #534 =org-canvas-validate= checks the dates of each row of an
  assignment's =#+NAME: overrides= table as it checks a heading's: a
  warning when a row's Unlock At comes after its Due At, or its Due At
  after its Lock At.  Each date is optional, and the cells are read as
  the override push reads them, in the course time zone.
* Changed
- #534 Date-order warnings compare the dates that are there: a heading
  whose =UNLOCK_AT= comes after its =LOCK_AT= warns when it has no
  =DUE_AT= between them, and a pair two of a discussion's date
  sequences share is reported once.
