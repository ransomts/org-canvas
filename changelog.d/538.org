* Added
- #538 =org-canvas-validate= accepts a finding a course means to keep,
  with the reason written beside it.  =:VALIDATE_ACCEPT: lock-window
  In-class exam: locks at the end of class= on a heading accepts that
  check there (several as =VALIDATE_ACCEPT+= lines); an =Accept= column
  in the overrides table does the same for a row, several separated by
  =;=.  The checks that can be accepted are =date-order=, =lock-window=
  and =require-unlock=.  An accepted finding leaves the report and is
  counted in one line; =org-canvas-validate-all= and =validate --all=
  list them with their reasons.  An unknown check, an accept with no
  reason or one naming an error accepts nothing and is a warning; an
  accept that matched nothing is a note.  =VALIDATE_ACCEPT= is never
  sent or pulled, the push ignores the =Accept= column, and a pull of
  the assignment keeps each Accept cell on its row, as =extend= does on the
  row it gives new dates.
- #538 =validate --json= adds =accepted= beside =suppressed=, a =check=
  on each finding, and =accepted_findings=, filled under =--all=.
* Changed
- #538 Validate reads an override row's dates as Canvas does: a date the
  row leaves empty is the assignment's.  A row moving the due date past
  the assignment's lock date, with no lock date of its own, is now a
  date-order warning; a pair of dates the row inherits is left to the
  heading.
