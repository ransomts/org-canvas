- #380 A push of one assignment heading — at point, by name with
  =org-canvas-sync-assignment=, or through =org-canvas-sync-headings= —
  also reconciles that heading's =#+NAME: overrides= table, and only
  that one, once the assignment went through. The by-heading result
  carries =:overrides= (the created, updated and deleted counts, or
  =failed=) and its closing line says so; before, the push reported
  =synced= while the section dates students see were unchanged.
- #380 An override sync no longer PUTs an override that already carries
  its row's dates, so a whole-file =org-canvas-sync-overrides= writes
  only the rows that changed.
