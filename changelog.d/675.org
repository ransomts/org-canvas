* Added
- #675 =org-canvas-validate= reads each published assignment not yet due against the modules in =modules.org= that hold it, since Canvas keeps it locked while its module is, whatever its own dates say. A module that unlocks at or after the due date is a warning; =org-canvas-validate-min-reach-days= (off by default) also warns when the column is first reachable, at the later of its own and its module's unlock, fewer days before it is due than the course wants, and =org-canvas-validate-reach-groups= narrows that to the assignment groups named. A module waiting on prerequisites is a note under the rule. The findings are check =module-reach=, which =VALIDATE_ACCEPT= can keep with a reason.
