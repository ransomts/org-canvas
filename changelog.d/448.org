* Added
- #448 =org-canvas-submissions-apply-ruling= applies a grading ruling to
  a list of students in a grading file, from Lisp or a batch run: every
  Rubric row's score set to a number or emptied, one fixed row comment
  (or none), SCORE set to a number or a function of the rows' total and
  the column's points, a drafted submission comment, and a dated Notes
  item recording the ruling's reason and the SCORE, rows, row comments
  and draft it replaced.  It returns what it changed, the ids that
  matched no student and the students kept back (one who left, one
  without a Rubric table, a SCORE the rubric total would contradict on
  a column graded by its rubric).  Nothing is sent; the grade push (S)
  sends it.  =scripts/org-canvas apply-ruling FILE RULING.json= takes
  one ruling or an array of them, a score of
  ={"deduct_percent": 20}= for the rows' total less 20% of the column,
  and exits 1 when an id matched nothing or a student was kept back.
