#+TITLE: Messages

Messages to students, sent through the Canvas Inbox with
=org-canvas-send-messages=.  The heading is the subject, the body is the
message, and =TO= says who gets it.  A sent heading is stamped and never
sent again: add a new heading to send again.  Sending is never part of
=org-canvas-sync=.

* Welcome to DS 101
:PROPERTIES:
:TO: Self
:END:
Welcome!  Course materials are in Modules, and office hours are on the
calendar.  Try a message on yourself first: TO: Self sends it to the
token's owner, nobody else.
